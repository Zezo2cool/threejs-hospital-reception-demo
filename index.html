How to explore

Desktop

  • Drag to rotate the view.
  • Scroll to zoom.
  • Right-drag to pan.
  • Click a colored marker to view information.

Touch

  • Drag with one finger to rotate.
  • Pinch to zoom.
  • Drag with two fingers to pan.
  • Tap a marker to view information.

Use Reset view to return to the starting position.
